Vivido Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Vivido is a voice journal. Your memories belong to you: they're kept on your iPhone and, if you choose, in your own iCloud Drive. We don't collect, see or sell any of them.

  • No account. You never sign up or sign in.
  • No tracking, analytics or ads.
  • Nothing is sent to us. We don't run any servers that receive your data.

What Vivido keeps, and where

Each memory is a pair of files on your iPhone: the recording (an audio file) and its details (a text file). The details include when you recorded it, the transcript of your words, a title and summary, labels such as people, places and topics, and, if you allowed them, the place, what you were doing (walking, driving…), the weather, and links to photos taken around that moment with small preview copies.

You can see these files in the Files app under On My iPhone › Vivido, export them, or delete them in the app.

iCloud backup

By default, Vivido keeps a copy of your memories in your own iCloud Drive (iCloud Drive › Vivido), private ones included, so you can restore them on a new iPhone. These copies are stored in your iCloud account under Apple's terms, and we can't access them. You can turn the backup off in Vivido's Settings › Backup. Deleting a memory in Vivido also removes its iCloud copy.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record your memories.
  • Speech recognition: to turn your recordings into text. This happens on your iPhone.
  • Location: only while you're recording, to note where a memory happened. Never in the background.
  • Motion: to note whether you were walking, running, cycling or driving.
  • Photos (optional): to link photos taken around a memory and keep a small copy with it.
  • Notifications (optional): for the evening question.

Services Vivido uses

To name a place and look up the weather for a memory, Vivido sends that memory's location, rounded to about 1 km, and its date to Apple's map service (for the place name) and to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com, for the weather). They use it only to answer the request, and it isn't linked to you. If you're offline, these are filled in later.

If you test Vivido through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with us, under Apple's TestFlight terms.

Children

Vivido isn't directed at children under 13.
